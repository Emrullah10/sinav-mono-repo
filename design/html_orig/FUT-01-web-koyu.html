Semiconductor Efficiency in Extreme Temperature Environments

Recent empirical investigations demonstrate that wide-bandgap semiconductors, particularly silicon carbide and gallium nitride, retain operational integrity under severe thermal fluctuations. The study establishes that the intrinsic charge carrier concentration remains suppressed, preventing premature thermal breakdown that typically deteriorates conventional silicon-based architectures.
